# Workflow 06 - Reporting

## Objective

Measure the performance of published content and recommend improvements.

## Input

- Published posts
- Platform analytics

## Process

1. Collect performance metrics.
2. Compare results against previous posts.
3. Identify the best-performing content.
4. Identify areas for improvement.
5. Generate a weekly performance summary.

## Output

One weekly marketing performance report with actionable recommendations.
