# Workflow 02 - Content Generation

## Objective

Generate high-quality educational content based on the approved topic.

## Input

- Approved topic from Workflow 01
- TeamSeafarers AI Marketing Brain

## Process

1. Read the approved topic.
2. Understand the target audience.
3. Generate valuable content.
4. Follow the 80% Value / 20% TeamSeafarers rule.
5. Prepare the content for review.

## Output

One complete draft ready for content review.
