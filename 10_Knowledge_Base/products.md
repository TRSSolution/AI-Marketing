# TeamSeafarers — Products & Platform Overview

## What TeamSeafarers Offers Maritime Institutes
- A platform to list and manage their courses online
- A way to receive confirmed online bookings from seafarers
- Tools to manage admissions with less manual effort
- Tools to track and manage payments
- Better online visibility for their courses
- Batch management support
- Optional discount configuration on courses
- Visibility into seafarer feedback and ratings

## What TeamSeafarers Offers Seafarers
- A way to discover maritime courses from multiple institutes in one place
- Search and filtering by location, institute, start date, and fee
- The ability to book courses online
- Access to course, institute, batch, and availability information in one place
- The ability to track their bookings and payments
- The ability to rate and review completed courses
- Notifications for booking, payment, and course updates
- A support ticket system for raising issues

## Main Business Outcomes
- Increased booking opportunities for institutes
- Reduced manual admissions work
- More organized payment tracking
- Time saved for institute staff
- A simpler course discovery and booking experience for seafarers
- Better-informed course choices for seafarers through ratings and reviews

## Major Platform Capabilities (High Level)
- Course discovery and search/filtering
- Online course booking
- Admissions management for institutes
- Payment tracking
- Batch/course scheduling management
- Discounted course pricing (where offered)
- Ratings and feedback on completed courses
- Support ticket system
- Notifications for bookings and course updates
- Invoice generation for bookings
- Web access and an Android mobile app for seafarers
