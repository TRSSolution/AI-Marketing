# TeamSeafarers — Policies Overview (Business Level)

## Seat Reservation
A seafarer reserves a seat by completing the required minimum payment or full payment. A temporary unpaid booking or payment attempt may expire if payment is not completed within the permitted time, after which the seat becomes available again.
Needs business confirmation: current minimum-payment requirement. Fee information is maintained in payments.md.

## Balance Payment Deadline (Freeze Date)
Each batch has an applicable freeze date or payment deadline. A seafarer who has made the minimum payment must complete the remaining balance by this deadline. If the required balance is not paid by the applicable deadline, the booking may be cancelled according to the current policy.
Needs business confirmation: the current freeze-date configuration and booking availability rules around the freeze date.

## Booking Journey (Business Level)
At a high level, a booking moves from payment and seat reservation to confirmation, course participation, and completion. A booking may instead be cancelled if payment requirements are not completed or if an eligible cancellation is requested.

## Seafarer Cancellation Eligibility
- A seafarer can cancel an eligible paid booking before the applicable freeze date.
- Online cancellation by the seafarer is not available after the freeze date.
- Refund eligibility and deductions depend on the cancellation policy applicable to the booking.

## Cancellation Deductions and Refunds
Refund eligibility and amounts follow the cancellation deduction and refund rules maintained in payments.md.

## Cancellation on an Institute's Request
Under the currently implemented process, an authorized TeamSeafarers administrator can cancel an eligible booking on an institute's request. The seafarer receives a full refund under this process, subject to the applicable eligibility conditions.
Needs business confirmation: the current eligibility deadline for cancellation on an institute's request.

## Course Start and Booking Completion
When the course begins, the associated eligible bookings move into the course-in-progress stage. After the course ends, they move to completion, and seafarers may provide feedback about their course experience.
