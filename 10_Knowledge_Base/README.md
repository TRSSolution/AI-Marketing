# TeamSeafarers Knowledge Base — Usage Guide (AI Marketing)

This folder contains the approved business reference for TeamSeafarers AI marketing work. Confirmed information in these six files is the source of truth. Information marked Needs business confirmation must not be treated as confirmed or used in published marketing claims.

## Which File to Use

- **company.md** — Company identity and positioning.
- **products.md** — High-level platform offering and capabilities.
- **institute.md** — Primary audience: institute problems, usage, and value.
- **seafarer.md** — Secondary audience: seafarer needs and value.
- **payments.md** — High-level financial and payment information.
- **policies.md** — Booking and cancellation policies.

## Audience Priority

Institutes are the primary audience. Seafarers are the secondary audience. Marketing content should primarily focus on helping maritime institutes increase bookings, consistent with `company.md` and `institute.md`.

## Rules for AI Marketing Employees

- Treat these six files as the approved business reference. Do not invent facts, features, fees, percentages, deadlines, timelines, guarantees, or policies not found here.
- Never publish information marked `Needs business confirmation` as a confirmed claim. If marketing content requires that information, flag it for human confirmation instead of guessing.
- Do not present planned, proposed, or unimplemented features as current capabilities. In particular, the proposed institute-portal partial-refund workflow is not an existing feature and must not be described as one.
- Exact financial and policy claims (fees, percentages, deadlines, timelines) require current human verification before publication, even if a similar figure appears elsewhere.
- Keep all content practical, truthful, and benefit-focused, and consistent with the relevant Knowledge Base file above.

## Unresolved Business-Confirmation Categories

The following categories currently contain information marked `Needs business confirmation` across `payments.md` and `policies.md`. Do not guess these; flag them for human confirmation before using them in marketing claims.

1. Minimum-payment requirements
2. Institute payout eligibility and timeline
3. Fee rates, taxes, and configurability
4. Cancellation and refund rules
5. Freeze-date and booking-availability rules
6. Eligibility deadline for cancellation on an institute's request
