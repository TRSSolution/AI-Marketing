# TeamSeafarers — Seafarer Overview

## Who Seafarer Users Are
Seafarers seeking maritime training courses. They are TeamSeafarers' secondary audience.

## Main Needs When Searching for Courses
- Find maritime courses across multiple institutes in one place
- See course, institute, batch, and availability information
- Search and filter courses by city, state, institute, and start date
- Sort courses by fee or start date, and filter for discounted courses
- Book a course online

## Common Problems
- Course information is scattered across different institute websites, social media pages, and WhatsApp groups
- Seafarers may need to contact multiple institutes to check course details, dates, and availability
- Difficulty comparing available courses and batches in one place
- Manual effort involved in booking and tracking payments

## How Seafarers Use TeamSeafarers (High Level)
- Discover maritime courses from multiple institutes
- Search and filter courses to find suitable options
- Complete their profile before booking a course
- View course, institute, batch, and availability information
- Book courses online
- Track their bookings and payments
- View and download invoices for their bookings
- Rate and review completed courses
- Raise and track support tickets for booking, payment, or technical issues
- Receive notifications for booking updates, payment confirmations, and course reminders
- Access TeamSeafarers through the web platform or Android mobile app

## Value TeamSeafarers Provides
- A single place to discover courses instead of checking institutes individually
- A simpler, online booking experience
- Visibility into their own bookings and payments
- Ratings and reviews from other seafarers to help choose courses
- Timely updates through notifications and support when issues arise
