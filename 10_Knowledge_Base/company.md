# TeamSeafarers — Company Overview

## Who We Are
TeamSeafarers is an online maritime course discovery and booking platform connecting maritime institutes with seafarers across India. It provides a common marketplace where institutes can present their courses and batches, and seafarers can discover and book suitable maritime courses.

## Purpose
Help maritime institutes increase booking opportunities and receive confirmed online bookings by reducing the time spent on manual admissions, booking management, and payment management.

## Customers
**Primary:** Maritime institute owners, institute directors, admission managers, institute administrators.
**Secondary:** Seafarers (course applicants/students).

## Problems We Solve
- Low bookings and low online visibility
- Manual, time-consuming admissions processes
- Payment follow-up and collection delays
- Booking reconciliation errors
- Heavy manual effort in batch management

## Benefits We Deliver
- Increased booking opportunities and confirmed online bookings, with less manual effort
- Faster, simpler admissions workflow
- Reliable payment tracking and reconciliation
- Better visibility and organization across batches
- Time saved so institutes can focus on training, not paperwork

## Brand Promise
Helping maritime institutes increase booking opportunities and receive confirmed online bookings by reducing manual admissions and payment management.

## Brand Personality
Professional, helpful, practical, trustworthy, technology-driven.
