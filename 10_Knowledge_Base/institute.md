# TeamSeafarers — Institute Overview

## Who Institute Users Are
- Maritime institute owners
- Institute directors
- Admission managers
- Institute administrators

## Main Goal
Increasing course bookings.

## Common Business Problems
- Low bookings
- Manual admissions processes
- Payment follow-up and collection delays
- Booking reconciliation errors
- Low online visibility
- Batch management effort

## How Institutes Use TeamSeafarers (High Level)
- Complete institute onboarding and profile setup before listing courses and batches
- List and manage their courses on the platform
- Configure course discounts and delivery modes, such as online, offline, or hybrid
- Receive bookings from seafarers online
- Manage admissions through the platform
- Track and manage payments
- Manage batches
- View and manage enrolled seafarers per batch, with data export
- View seafarer feedback and ratings for their courses
- View payout settlement invoices
- Raise and track support tickets with the TeamSeafarers team

## Value TeamSeafarers Provides
- Increased booking opportunities
- Less manual admissions work
- More organized payment tracking
- Better online visibility for courses
- Time saved for institute staff
- Insight into seafarer feedback to help improve course offerings
