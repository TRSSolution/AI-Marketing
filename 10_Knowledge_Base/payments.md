# TeamSeafarers — Payments Overview (Business Level)

## How Seafarers Pay for Course Bookings
Seafarers pay for course bookings online through TeamSeafarers using the available online payment methods. The amount payable may include the course fee, convenience fee, and applicable taxes.

## Minimum, Balance, and Full Payment Options
- **Minimum Payment**: Where this option is available, a seafarer can pay the required minimum amount toward the course fee to reserve a seat.
- **Balance Payment**: After making the minimum payment, the seafarer must pay the remaining course fee by the applicable payment deadline or freeze date.
- **Full Payment**: A seafarer can pay the full course fee required for the booking in one payment.
Needs business confirmation: current minimum-payment requirement and whether it is uniformly available or configurable.

## How Institutes Track Booking Payments
Institutes can view the payment status of bookings in their batches, including amounts paid and any balance due. They can also view payout information and settlement invoices for eligible bookings.

## How TeamSeafarers Settles Payments with Institutes
TeamSeafarers collects booking payments from seafarers and settles the eligible amount with institutes after deducting the applicable service fee and other authorized adjustments.
Needs business confirmation: current payout eligibility rules and settlement timeline.

## Convenience Fee and Service Fee
- **Convenience Fee**: An additional fee added to each seafarer payment; this is paid by the seafarer.
- **Service Fee**: An amount deducted from course fees before the balance is settled to the institute; this is borne by the institute.
Needs business confirmation: current fee rates, applicable taxes, and whether the fees are fixed or configurable.

## Invoices
- Seafarers can view and download invoices for their course payments.
- Institutes can view and download payout invoices for their settlements.

## High-Level Financial Impact of Cancellations and Refunds
- The refundable amount depends on the applicable cancellation policy and the timing and reason for cancellation.
- Applicable cancellation deductions may be taken before the refund is issued.
- Some fees, such as the convenience fee, may be non-refundable according to the current policy.
Needs business confirmation: current cancellation deductions, fee refundability, refund eligibility rules, and processing timeline.
