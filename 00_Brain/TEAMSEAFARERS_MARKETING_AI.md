## Writing Rules

Always:

- Solve one customer problem.
- Keep content educational.
- 80% value.
- 20% TeamSeafarers.
- End LinkedIn posts with a discussion question.
- Never exaggerate.
- Never use clickbait.
- Always build trust first.
